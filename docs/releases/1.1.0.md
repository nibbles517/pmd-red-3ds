*Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team* on the 3DS, with new features and fixes.

**No game data is included.** As before, put your own ROM of *Red Rescue Team*
(**USA**), named `pmdred.gba`, next to `pmd-red-3ds.3dsx` or in
`SD:/3ds/pmd-red-3ds/`. See the [README](README.md) for setup and features.

## New

- **Music Box:** using it in a dungeon plays its tune, then lets you pick a DS
  song to play in place of the floor's music: from Blue Rescue Team, Explorers of
  Sky, or any other DS game's ROM on your SD card. It's no longer used up; the
  song plays until the Music Box leaves your Toolbox or the dungeon ends.
- **Run option: Ginseng → PP Up.** Ginseng becomes PP Up: each adds 1 max PP to a
  move, up to five per move, shown as stars after the move's PP. At Gulpin's Link
  Shop, a move's menu has **Upgrade**: a PP Up for 10000 Poké.
- **Run option: Tutor Moves.** At Gulpin's Link Shop, a Pokémon's menu has
  **Special Moves**: its Generation III egg and tutor moves (15000 Poké) and event
  and Colosseum/XD moves (25000 Poké).

Run options are picked when you start a New Game.

## Fixed

- Clouds in the overworld and the light at Stratos Lookout no longer flicker in
  sizes other than GBA.
- The Personality Test's wavy background cycles its colors properly with GPU
  Drawing on.
- A sprite slot leak with GPU Drawing on (a possible cause of corrupted item
  sprites in busy dungeons).
- Cutscenes: Pokémon placed better in the wider sizes in more scenes of the story.
